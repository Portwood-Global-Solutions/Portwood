## Licensing and portal access

In the subscriber sandbox, **Setup → Installed Packages → Portwood → Manage Licenses** showed two allowed licenses and two used. The two assignments belonged to a staff user and the signing site’s guest user. On the guest user’s record, **Managed Packages** showed Portwood as active and **Permission Set Assignments** included **Portwood Guest Signature**. The public-link example uses the published standard rate of $13 USD per user per month. Five staff and one signing guest total $78 per month. Portwood’s Founder’s Site License is quoted per org and includes the guest user. Portwood’s product owner confirmed that signing as an authenticated employee or portal member requires that site license. The sandbox had per-user assignments. It verified the setup screens, while the purchasing rule comes from the product owner.
